# Privacy Policy for Ring Sort Master **Last updated:** October 29, 2026 **Ring Sort Master** ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed and provided as an ad-supported mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using our app, and the choices you have associated with that data. By downloading, installing, or playing Ring Sort Master, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. --- ## 1. Information Collection and Storage ### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device Ring Sort Master stores your gameplay progress strictly on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore: - Unlocked level progress and level completion states (Classic and Timed modes) - Star ratings earned per level (1–3 stars) - Move counts and best completion times per level - Game preferences (sound toggles, haptics, theme selections, color-blind indicators) - Hint and Undo inventory balances - Daily challenge streak counts We do NOT transmit your personal gameplay progress, names, or scores to external servers. All gameplay data remains on your physical device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. ### B. Device and Advertising Information (Collected by Third Parties) Because Ring Sort Master is free and ad-supported, we integrate third-party services that may automatically collect certain diagnostic and advertising identifiers: - Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID) - Approximate location (country or coarse IP-level location) - Device model, operating system version, and screen resolution - Ad interaction statistics (impressions, clicks, video completion) - Crash data and app performance metrics --- ## 2. Third-Party Service Providers Our application integrates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) to serve advertisements and manage privacy consent: - **Google AdMob & Google Mobile Ads SDK**: Used to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. - Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy - Google Advertising Technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads - **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)**: Used to comply with regional privacy regulations (such as GDPR in the European Economic Area/UK, and US state privacy laws) by presenting users with consent dialogs for personalized advertising. These third parties have their own privacy policies governing their use of your data. --- ## 3. Offline Gameplay & Data Control - **Offline Mode:** Ring Sort Master is fully playable without an active internet connection. When played offline, no ad requests are transmitted and no advertising data is collected. - **Resetting Your Advertising ID:** You can reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your Android device settings: - Go to **Settings** > **Privacy** > **Ads** > **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**. --- ## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Policy) Ring Sort Master is designed for a general audience of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete such data. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at the email address listed below. --- ## 5. Security We value your trust in providing us your information. We do not maintain remote user databases or collect sensitive personal credentials. Third-party advertising partners utilize industry-standard cryptographic protocols (such as HTTPS/TLS) to protect data in transit. --- ## 6. Permissions Used by the App The App only requests permissions strictly necessary for its functionality: - `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required to load ads from Google AdMob and check network availability. - `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required to verify whether the device is online or offline for uninterrupted gameplay. - `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used solely for in-game tactile haptic feedback during ring movements and button taps (can be toggled in Settings). We do NOT request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise GPS location, or external device storage. --- ## 7.
